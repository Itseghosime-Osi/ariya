      How it works

      Three steps from list to last dance.

      1. Upload your guest list

        Import a spreadsheet or paste names and numbers. We flag missing details and duplicates before anything is saved.

      2. Send personal links

        Each guest gets their own link by WhatsApp, SMS or email. They reply in seconds, with no app to install, and get a confirmation email.

      3. Seat and check in

        Confirmed guests fill your seating plan. Vendors work from the same checklist, and on the day you know exactly who's coming.
