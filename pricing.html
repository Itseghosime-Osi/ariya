Questions

Before you start.

Do guests need an app?

No. Each guest opens their personal link in any phone browser and replies in a tap.

Can planners use it for several clients?

Yes. The Planner plan is charged per client event, so you only pay for the events you run.

What happens when a table is full?

Àríyá won't seat more people than a table has chairs, and shows how many seats are left before you assign anyone.

Is it ready to use today?

Àríyá is a prototype. The planner runs on sample data so you can try every step; accounts, sending and payments are being built.
