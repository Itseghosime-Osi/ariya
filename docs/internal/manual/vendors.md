# Vendors and budget

One roster of vendors, their tasks counted back from the day, and a budget strip.

Audience: Hosts and planners

> Archived from the former public manual. Internal reference only; content reflects the product at the time it was archived.

`/events/[id]/vendors` holds each vendor with contact details, a status and tasks. Tasks have due dates counted back from the event date, so overdue ones rise to the top and ticking one off is one tap.

## Categories

- Catering & Drinks; Photography & Videography; Decor & Floral
- DJ, Live Band & Sound; Master of Ceremonies (MC); Security & Bouncers
- Traditional Attire & Aso-Ebi; Cake & Desserts; Hair & Makeup
- Stationery & Calligraphy; Logistics & Coordination; Other

## Status

A vendor moves through **draft → contracted → confirmed → completed**. Costs and deposits feed the budget weave and the deposits ring on the overview.

> Note: Vendors are searchable from `⌘K`. The vendor page uses the same desk look as the rest of the app.
