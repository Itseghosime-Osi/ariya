# Ten-minute tour

From a blank account to a guest holding their personal invitation.

Audience: New hosts

> Archived from the former public manual. Internal reference only; content reflects the product at the time it was archived.

1. **Create an account** at `/sign-up`, with email and password or **Continue with Google**. Tick the Terms and Privacy box.
2. **Create a celebration** from `/events` → New. Give it a title, the couple's names, date, venue and area.
3. **Add guests**: type them in, import a spreadsheet (CSV), or pick people from your phone's contacts or a `.vcf` file. See [Guests](guests.md).
4. **Design the invitation** (Invitations → Open the designer). Start in Simple mode, or import your own card. See [Designer](designer.md).
5. **Send**: tick the guests, choose Email and send. For WhatsApp and SMS, copy the personal link and paste it into the chat. See [Send invitations](invitations.md).
6. **Watch replies arrive** on the event overview. Seat the people who said yes in [Seating](seating.md).
7. **On the day**, open the [check-in desk](check-in.md), scan or type each guest's code, and tap Admit.

> Free up to 20 guests: Small gatherings (up to 20 invited guests) are free. Bigger ones need one Celebration Pass (₦50,000 per event). See [Billing](billing.md).

## Try it with demo data

On a local install, `pnpm db:seed` creates a demo host (`demo@owambe.ng`) and a celebration with 60 guests, 7 banquet tables and 6 vendors. See [Local setup](setup.md).
