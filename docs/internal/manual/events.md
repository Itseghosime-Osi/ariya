# Celebrations

Create, run, archive and share a celebration, and what the overview shows.

Audience: Hosts and planners

> Archived from the former public manual. Internal reference only; content reflects the product at the time it was archived.

## Creating one

Go to `/events` and choose **New**. A celebration has a title, the couple's names, a date, a venue and area, a dress code, a description, a status, a visibility setting and an RSVP deadline. Every one of these can be changed later in Settings or the Page editor.

## The overview

`/events/[eventId]` is the home of a celebration. A banner holds the wax seal (the couple's initials), names, one detail line and one big number. Below it:

- **Readiness rings**: one ring each for replies, seating, vendors, deposits and tasks. Hover or focus a ring to see its number.
- **Guest constellation**: stars grouped by side, with filters and an expected-on-the-day stepper.
- **Reply waffle**: 100 dots, one per percent of replies (accepted, awaiting, declined).
- **Budget weave**: a strip of bands sized by cost, solid when paid, hatched when not.
- **Road to the day** and **Guestbook**, plus a preview of the saved invitation (the same renderer the guest sees).

Every graphic has its numbers as text too, so nothing depends on colour alone.

## Your celebrations list

`/events` shows an interactive stage of upcoming celebrations, guest replies, this week's items, recent replies, a draggable to-do board and an All events ledger. Cards open into the detail page with a shared-element transition.

## Settings, archive and expiry

- **Settings** (`/events/[id]/settings`) edits the core details.
- **Archive** makes a celebration read-only. An archived celebration's hall and seating cannot be changed, even by an AI proposal.
- **Expiry** is calculated from the event date by `event-expiry`; old events are treated as finished.
