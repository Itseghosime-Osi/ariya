# Brand touches in the studio: a proposal (K11)

Checklist K11 asks for Àríyá colours and motifs "used sparingly (accent, empty states, loading), not on every control". Whether that is restrained enough is a taste call, so this is a short proposal for the owner to accept or change. The owner said yes on 2026-11-13 and all three are built.

## Where the brand shows today

- **Colour:** wine (`--studio-wine`) is the one solid primary action per state (K1 proves exactly one). Gold (`--studio-gold`) is the focus ring in dark mode and the selected-state accent. Everything else is neutral paper, ink and mauve.
- **Motif:** the wax-seal monogram appears as a tool and on cards; the card itself carries the house ribbon and border (Decoration, now under "More options").
- **Loading:** the studio skeleton is neutral grey pulses.

## Proposed touches (three, no more)

| #   | Where                       | Touch                                                                  | Why it is safe                                        |
| --- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Empty canvas (a blank card) | A faint wax-seal outline above "Add your first words", in mauve at 12% | Shows once, then disappears the moment a layer exists |
| 2   | Loading skeleton            | The seal outline pulsing in place of the middle grey block             | Already animated; stops with reduced motion           |
| 3   | Export finished             | A single gold check on the "Exported" notice, no confetti              | One small mark at the moment of delight               |

## What stays plain on purpose

Tool rail, inspector, layers, history, dialogs and every button other than the one primary action. A second accent colour on controls would compete with the card, which is the thing the host is making.

## Decision

Yes, all three (2026-11-13). Built: the seal outline on the empty card and in the loading skeleton, and the check on the export notice.
