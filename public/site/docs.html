How Àríyá works

One place for every yes, every seat and every vendor — instead of fifty group chats. We will keep adding to these guides; this page lays out the shape.

The calm behind the party.

Àríyá runs the logistics of a Nigerian celebration: who is invited, who replied, where they sit, and which vendors still owe you something. Hosts and planners see the same live headcount, and guests only ever see their own invitation.

i

Nothing here is sent anywhere. The samples on this page use pretend guests — your dashboard uses your own events. See what Àríyá does for the full tour.

From list to last dance.

  1. Upload your guest listImport a spreadsheet or paste names and numbers. Missing details and duplicates are flagged before anything is saved.
  2. Send personal linksEach guest gets their own link by WhatsApp, SMS or email, and a confirmation email when they reply.
  3. Seat and check inConfirmed guests fill your seating plan, vendors work from one checklist, and on the day you know exactly who is coming.

Each invitation is personal: their name, your colours of the day, the date and venue. Guests open it in any phone browser — no app to install — and reply in one tap.

Every yes gets a chair.

Confirmed guests fill a plan of your hall, table by table. Pick a guest, choose a table, and Àríyá keeps the count.

Ushers look guests up by name and see their table instantly. Arrivals tick off against the same list you planned with, so the headcount stays honest from first dance to last.

Pay per event. No subscription.

Small gatherings of up to 20 guest invitations are free. Anything bigger takes one Celebration Pass of ₦50,000 per event — planners pay the same pass for each client event they run.

Before you start.

Do guests need an app?

No. Each guest opens their personal link in any phone browser and replies in a tap.

Can planners use it for several clients?

Yes. Planners use the same Celebration Pass, charged per client event, so you only pay for the events you run.

What happens when a table is full?

Àríyá will not seat more people than a table has chairs, and shows how many seats are left before you assign anyone.

Is it ready to use today?

You can create an account and plan your event. The examples on our public pages use sample data; your dashboard uses your own events. Check the payment mode shown at checkout before paying.
