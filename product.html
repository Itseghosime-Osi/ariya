What Àríyá does

The calm behind the party.

Àríyá is OwambePlan's planner for couples and the planners who run their day. Your guest list, invitations, replies, seats and vendors live in one place instead of fifty group chats.

Invitations and RSVP

Every guest gets their own link.

  • A personal invitationTheir name, your colours of the day, the date and venue. Shared by WhatsApp, SMS or email.
  • One-tap repliesAccept or decline, with a plus-one only when you allow it. No app to install.
  • A confirmation emailGuests get their reply, date, venue and table in writing.

    How it works

    Three steps from list to last dance.

    1. Upload your guest list

      Import a spreadsheet or paste names and numbers. Missing details and duplicates are flagged before anything is saved.

    2. Send personal links

      Each guest gets their own link by WhatsApp, SMS or email, and a confirmation email when they reply.

    3. Seat and check in

      Confirmed guests fill your seating plan, vendors work from one checklist, and on the day you know exactly who's coming.
